Help & Support

Summit Ridgeline for Android · Last updated 11 October 2026

Get in touch

Write to support@cypresstech.net. Please include the app name, the version from Settings → About, your phone model and what happened.

How do I climb? There is no up button.

Height is earned. LEFT and RIGHT run. JUMP jumps from a ledge, grabs a ledge or a rope while you are in the air, pulls you up when you are hanging, and lets go of a rope so your swing carries you. Run at a gap for the longest jump.

What happens when I fall?

Nothing is lost. You start again at the last flag you reached. Flags are saved as you touch them.

What do the levers do?

Walk to a lever and it slides a stone slab across the gap ahead of it.

What is the bar on the left?

Your progress to the summit. The marks are flags; the dot is you.

What does the purchase unlock?

One purchase removes the ads and unlocks the Hard difficulty and the taller 500 m and 1000 m mountains. It is not a subscription.

I bought it but still see ads, or I have a new phone.

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the Google account you bought it with. If the price does not show, check that you are online and that Google Play is up to date.

Can I play left-handed?

Yes. Settings → Controls swaps the buttons to the other side.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Settings → Sound.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every part of the game works with no connection. Ads simply do not appear.

Where are my climbs kept?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy, so they do not move to a new phone and are erased if you uninstall.

How do I change my ad consent?

Where a consent choice applies to you, Settings → Privacy choices opens the form again.
